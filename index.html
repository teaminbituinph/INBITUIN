Who is Inbituin?

INBITUIN is a 4-member pre-debut genderless P-Pop group officially introduced in June 2026. The group aims to showcase individuality, artistry, and inclusivity through music and performance. As they prepare for their debut, they continue to gain attention for their unique concept and talented members.

Fordians Official

FORDIANS Wordplay: “For” + “Radiance”

FOR (Purpose): Fans exist for Ford, and Inbituin’s music is created for the fans. It represents a mutual dedication.

DIANS (Radiance / Diamonds): The suffix closely mimics words associated with light, like radiance or iridescence. A Fordian is someone who reflects Ford’s light and brings their own brilliance to the fandom.

Miggysaurus Official

Miggysaurus = Miggy + saurus 🦕

“Miggy” comes from his name, while “saurus” is inspired by Brachiosaurus, represented by his dinosaur emoji. It’s a playful name that connects directly to Miggy and his love/association with dinosaurs.
